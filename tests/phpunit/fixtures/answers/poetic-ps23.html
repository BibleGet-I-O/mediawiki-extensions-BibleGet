NABRE

Psalms 23

1A psalm of David.
The LORD is my shepherd;
there is nothing I lack.2In green pastures he makes me lie down;
to still waters he leads me;3he restores my soul.
He guides me along right paths
for the sake of his name.4Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death,
I will fear no evil, for you are with me;
your rod and your staff comfort me.5You set a table before me
in front of my enemies;
You anoint my head with oil;
my cup overflows.6Indeed, goodness and mercy will pursue me
all the days of my life;
I will dwell in the house of the LORD
for endless days.
